## 📊 Data Source Documentation

This project utilizes a multi-location European retail pharmacy dataset designed as a star schema to evaluate sales performance, product profitability, and operational store metrics.

* **Dataset Sourced From:** Kaggle
* **Direct Download Link:** [EU Pharmacy Products and Pricing](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ehmadali/eu-pharmacy-products-and-pricing)
* **Maintained By:** Ehsan Ahmad Ali

---

### 📦 Dataset Properties & Footprint

* **Architecture Pattern:** Star Schema (1 Fact Table, 3 Dimension Tables)
* **Raw File Format:** Flat CSVs
* **Temporal Scope:** January 1, 2024 – December 31, 2025 (2 Full Fiscal Years)
* **Currency:** Euros (€ / EUR)
* **Total Scale:** 63,210 total rows across 4 relational tables
